Tujuan Pembelajaran

Physical security memainkan peran utama di setiap organisasi — proteksi critical information, network infrastructure, physical equipment, facilities, personnel dari environmental disasters, terrorism, vandalism, dan theft.

Physical security berperan utama di setiap organisasi. Ia mencakup proteksi critical information, network infrastructure, physical equipment dan devices, facilities, personnel, dll. dari environmental disasters, terrorism, vandalism, dan theft. Bagian 02

Need for Physical Security

Mengapa physical security adalah aspek paling overlooked — dan mengapa attacker mengeksploitasinya.

Meskipun cyber-attacks menjadi semakin complex, attacker terus menggunakan berbagai teknik untuk mengkompromi physical security organisasi. Namun, organisasi semakin fokus menguatkan IT security, yang overshadows physical security. Physical security adalah aspek keamanan yang paling overlooked, dan fakta ini telah disadari banyak organisasi dalam lima tahun terakhir. Mengetahui fakta ini, attacker mengambil keuntungan dari loopholes untuk mengkompromi physical security organisasi.

Menurut data US Department of Health and Human Services Breach Portal, physical security breaches adalah salah satu security incident yang paling sering terjadi di organisasi. Menurut BCI Scan Report, physical security kini dipersepsikan sebagai growing concern untuk business continuity professionals — termasuk possibility terrorism dan security incident seperti vandalism, theft, atau fraud.

Key Insight: Physical security breaches berbeda dari security breach lainnya. Mereka dapat dilakukan dengan little to no technical knowledge. Conventional security measures seperti firewalls dan IDSes tidak menjamin physical security — firewall bekerja di atas physical layer OSI model, sehingga tidak melindungi physical layer jaringan. Physical security harus diimplementasikan di physical layer (Layer 1) OSI model.

2.1 Physical Layer OSI — Apa yang Dilindungi

🔌
All Cabling & Network Systems

Semua kabel fisik — UTP, STP, fiber-optic, coaxial. Kabel yang dapat di-tap, di-cut, atau di-sabotage. Physical access ke kabel = akses ke data yang transit.

🖥
Physical Access to Systems

Akses fisik ke server, workstation, router, switch. Siapa yang bisa sentuh device = bisa install keylogger, clone disk, reset password via console.

⚡
Power Support

Power supply untuk semua kabel dan sistem. Tanpa power = tidak ada sistem. Power fluctuation = hardware damage. UPS & generator = critical.

🌡
Environment Support

Lingkungan yang menopang sistem — temperature, humidity, ventilation, fire suppression. Server room tanpa AC = overheating = hardware failure.

4.1 Location & Site Architecture Considerations

📍
Location Considerations

Sebelum buy/lease building, pertimbangkan: facility location, neighboring buildings, joint tenancy risks, power & water supply, sewage, proximity to roads/transportation, emergency support, fire stations, hospitals, airports, local crime rate, prior security incidents. Hindari lokasi prone to natural disasters (floods, tornadoes, earthquakes, hurricanes, mudslides, fires).

🏗
Site Architecture

Design considerations: number of entrances (main, staircase, parking, lift, hallway, reception), neighboring facilities analysis, assets inventory, critical infrastructure identification, separate server/storage room, emergency exits, environmental hazard management, sanitation system, parking away from main building.

Bagian 05

Physical Barriers

Multi-layer approach: external barriers (fences/walls), middle barriers (equipment), internal barriers (doors/windows).

Physical barriers define physical boundary suatu area dan membagi vehicle traffic dari pedestrians. Penggunaan physical barrier deters dan delays outsiders dari entering premises. Untuk discourage intruders, gunakan multi-layer approach: external barriers (fences, walls), middle barriers (equipment yang obstruct traffic), internal barriers (doors, windows, grills, glass, curtains).

5.1 Security Personnel

👮
Guards

👨‍✈️

👔

🎓

Bagian 06

Physical Locks

4 jenis locking systems: mechanical, digital, electric/electromagnetic, combination.

🔑
Mechanical Locks

Easy method to restrict unauthorized access. Dengan atau tanpa keys. 2 tipe: Warded lock (spring-loaded bolt + notch, only correct key fits) dan Tumbler lock (metal pieces in slot, correct key raises pieces above bolt). Tumbler: pin, disk, lever variants.

🔢
Digital Locks

Require fingerprints, smart cards, atau keypad PINs. Easy to handle, no keys to lose/forge. Provides automatic locking. User hanya swipe smart card, scan fingerprint, atau enter PIN.

⚡
Electric/Electromagnetic Locks

Operate on electric current. Locking/unlocking via supplying/eliminating power. Activated/deactivated via magnets atau motors. Fail safe (unlocked saat power loss) vs Fail secure (locked saat power loss). Electromagnetic = electromagnet + armature plate.

🔓
Combination Locks

Require combination of numbers & letters. Entry via keypad atau rotating dial yang intermingles dengan rotating discs. No keys needed. Keamanan: tergantung panjang & complexity combination.

Fail Safe vs Fail Secure: Pilihan tergantung use case. Fail safe untuk emergency exits (harus terbuka saat power loss agar orang bisa keluar). Fail secure untuk server room/data center (harus tetap terkunci saat power loss untuk mencegah unauthorized access). Kombinasi dengan UPS = best practice untuk fail secure.
Bagian 07

Concealed Weapon / Contraband Detection

Devices untuk detect illegal materials — explosives, bombs, weapons — sebelum masuk premises.

🚪
Walkthrough Metal Detectors

Di airport terminals, schools, sports stadiums. Check people yang admission ke certain areas. Harus maintained & properly monitored. Deploy di setiap entry point organisasi.

🔍
Handheld Metal Detectors

Screen people lebih closely, detect suspicious objects. Digunakan di most places where walkthrough detectors digunakan — sebagai secondary screening.

📡
X-ray Inspection Systems

Easy to handle. Use X-rays instead of visible light untuk screen objects — bags, packages, cargo. Dapat detect explosives, weapons, contraband bahkan saat covered by other objects.

Bagian 08

Mantrap — Interlocking Door System

Physical access security control untuk catching trespassers — separates non-secure from secure areas.

Mantrap adalah mechanical locking mechanism yang terdiri dari small space dengan two sets of interlocking doors. First set of doors must close sebelum second set opens. User authentication di mantrap doors menggunakan smart cards, keypad PINs, atau biometric verification. Mantrap beroperasi automatically, berguna untuk authorizing visitors, mengurangi manpower, dan menjamin safety organisasi.

Bagian 09

Surveillance & Lighting

4 jenis lighting systems untuk deter, detect, dan respond terhadap physical security threats.

9.1 Lighting Systems

💡
Continuous Lighting

Arranged untuk provide continuous lighting ke large area throughout the night. Primary deterrence — area well-lit discourages intruders.

🔦
Standby Lighting

Used whenever suspicious activity detected by security personnel atau alarm system. Operate manually atau automatically.

🔦
Movable Lighting

Manually controlled lighting system yang provides lighting at night atau only when needed. Normally used as extension of continuous atau standby system.

🚨
Emergency Lighting

Used mainly during power failures atau when regular lighting systems fail. Critical untuk safe evacuation + security saat blackout.

9.2 CCTV & Video Surveillance

📹
CCTV Systems

Guards generally handle CCTV cameras as deterrent dan mechanism to detect dan possibly prevent intrusion. 24×7 surveillance untuk sensitive areas (server rooms, entrance, parking).

🎥
Modern Surveillance

IP cameras with motion detection, night vision, analytics (face recognition, license plate recognition). Cloud recording + AI anomaly detection. Integration dengan access control system.

Power Supply — UPS Types

6 jenis UPS untuk handle blackouts, power outages, spikes, dan surges.

Facilities dapat menderita blackouts atau power outages yang membuat systems inoperable. Power outages bisa impact ability untuk provide IT services dan physical security. Power spikes, surges, atau blackouts bisa damage equipment. UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) memungkinkan computers berfungsi saat power failure — contains battery yang senses power fluctuations.

Bagian 11

Workplace Security

Reception area, server/backup security, critical assets, removable devices — semua aspek workplace.

🛎
Reception Area

Initial point of contact. Vulnerable karena provides easy access ke strangers. Receptionists harus recognize unusual behavior (solicitors, ex-employees). Maintain eye contact, non-confrontational posture. Important files tidak di reception desk. Computer screens tidak visible to visitors. Always locked saat receptionist away.

🖥
Server/Backup Security

Physical security untuk critical servers & backup devices: separate room, CCTV + smart card + biometric auth, rack mount servers, UPS attached, locked drawers/cabinets. Backup devices stored off-site. Full disk encryption. Motion sensing alarms. Discourage DVD/USB backups.

💻
Critical Assets

Workstations (unoccupied = vulnerable, disconnect/remove), routers/switches (locked rooms), printers (bolted down, separate locations). Network devices & computer equipment in locked cabinets — biometric locks + climate control.

📱
Removable & Portable Devices

USB, SD cards, laptops, phones — pose physical security risks. Keep in drawer/safe, cable lock permanently. Encrypt hard drives. Install anti-theft software (remote lock/track/wipe). Enable lockout after failed attempts. Label device with contact info. Docking station yang permanently fixes laptop.

Bagian 13

Physical Security Policy

Guidelines untuk ensure adequate physical security measures diimplementasikan.

Physical security policy mendefinisikan guidelines untuk memastikan adequate physical security measures diimplementasikan. Di IT organisations di mana large amounts physical assets ditangani, assets prone to damage during installations atau transfer. Care harus diambil dalam terms of frequency of monitoring dan analyzing risks, dan training provided to people handling physical assets. Designing physical security policy helps organisasi maintain norms yang diikuti employees untuk reduce probability of loss.

13.1 Design Considerations Checklist

📋
Building Protection

Is building protection deficiency reviewed regularly? Is there a process to identify outsiders (visitors, contractors, vendors) before granting access? Are adequate lighting systems installed?

🚪
Entry Points

Are each of the entry points properly blocked? Are badges, locks, keys, dan authentication controls audited regularly?

📹
Surveillance

Is video surveillance footage monitored regularly? Is the inventory of the organization's assets maintained regularly?

🔒
Physical Security Checklist

Follow copyright rules, store removable items in locker, keep sensitive areas under surveillance, advise employees to swipe card, avoid combustible materials, ensure company satisfaction, evaluate physical security of location.

Bagian 14

Environmental Controls

HVAC, EMI Shielding, dan Hot/Cold Aisles — kontrol lingkungan untuk protect hardware & data.

14.1 HVAC — Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning

Continuous power consumption/supply dapat menyebabkan data centers, hardware, dan equipment menjadi hot sangat cepat. Improper equipment placement dapat increase risk of fire. HVAC adalah special system yang controls environment di room atau building — terutama humidity conditions dan ventilation. Deployed untuk maintain comfortable temperatures agar hardware tidak terpengaruh moisture dan changes in air. HVAC juga purifies air, removes smoke, odor, heat, dan dust particles.

❄️
4 Types HVAC Systems

(1) Heating & AC Split System — most common, components inside & outside building. (2) Hybrid Heat Split — advanced, better energy efficiency, electric heat pump. (3) Duct-free Split — for locations where traditional can't be used. (4) Packaged — most efficient, all components in single unit.

🌡
HVAC Baseline

Baseline temperature dan humidity levels harus maintained untuk keep equipment working reliably. Equipment yang emits hot/cold air harus continuously monitored. Recommended: 18-27°C, 40-60% humidity untuk data center (ASHRAE guidelines).

14.2 EMI Shielding

📡
Electromagnetic Interference Shielding

Electromagnetic radiation dari different electronic devices interferes dengan surrounding devices, causes problems: shaky monitors, system failures, unexplained shutdowns. EMI shielding = practice of coating electronic equipment dengan metals agar electromagnetic waves tidak interfere. Uses metals atau metal foams. Conductor produces eddy currents yang cancel effect of magnetic field. Industries: telecommunication, healthcare (heavy equipment).

14.3 Hot & Cold Aisles

Hot & Cold Aisles — Data Center Airflow Management
Gambar 8. Hot & Cold Aisles — racks arranged so fronts face cold air (AC output), backs face hot air (AC input). Prevents mixing, increases efficiency.
Hot/Cold Aisle Benefits: Protects hardware dari humidity & heat, increases hardware performance, maintains consistent room temperature. Cold aisle: easy implement, less expensive, fits existing data center. Hot aisle: more effective, perfect air distribution, but expensive & uncomfortable for maintenance.

15.3 Workflow Praktikum 4 Sesi

Alur Praktikum — 4 Sesi, 20 Langkah, 1 Laporan Akhir
Gambar 9. Workflow 4 sesi praktikum Modul 04 — total 20 langkah hands-on.

15.4 Langkah Praktikum

Sesi 1 — Physical Security Audit

1
Prepare Audit Checklist

Buat checklist berdasarkan modul: (a) location considerations, (b) site architecture, (c) physical barriers (fences, doors, windows), (d) locks (type, condition), (e) surveillance (CCTV coverage, blind spots), (f) lighting (continuous, standby, emergency), (g) power supply (UPS, generator), (h) HVAC, (i) access control (badge, biometric, mantrap).

2
Conduct On-Site Audit

Dengan izin, audit gedung kampus/lab. Foto setiap area. Catat: number of entrances, type of locks, CCTV placement (cover or blind spot?), lighting adequacy, server room security (locked? biometric? CCTV?), reception area security, fire suppression system, emergency exits, UPS presence.

3
Identify Gaps & Risks

Untuk setiap area, identify: (a) gap (what's missing/non-compliant), (b) risk level (Critical/High/Medium/Low), (c) recommendation. Contoh: "Server room lock = mechanical only (gap), risk: HIGH, recommendation: upgrade to biometric + CCTV."

4
Multi-Layer Barrier Assessment

Assess apakah gedung punya 3-layer defense: External (fence, wall, bollard), Middle (turnstile, checkpoint, reception), Internal (locked doors, badge access, biometric). Identifikasi layer yang lemah atau hilang.

5
Laporan Audit

Buat laporan: executive summary, detailed findings per area dengan foto evidence, risk matrix (likelihood × impact), remediation roadmap (quick wins 90 hari, medium 6 bulan, long 1 tahun), budget estimate. Present ke class.

Sesi 2 — Access Control System dengan Raspberry Pi

1
Setup Hardware

Hubungkan: RFID RC522 ke Raspberry Pi SPI pins. Relay module ke GPIO pin. 12V electromagnetic lock ke relay. LED green (access granted) + LED red (denied) + buzzer. Power: 12V adapter untuk lock, 5V USB untuk Pi.

2
Write Python Access Control Script

Install spi-dev dan MFRC522 library. Script: (a) wait for RFID tag, (b) read UID, (c) check against whitelist di database (SQLite), (d) if match → trigger relay (unlock 3 detik) + green LED + log entry, (e) if no match → red LED + buzzer + log failed attempt.

3
Test dengan Multiple Tags

Register 3 RFID tags sebagai authorized, 2 sebagai unauthorized. Test: tap authorized → door unlock 3 detik. Tap unauthorized → buzzer + red LED. Test rapid tap → rate limiting (lockout after 5 failed).

4
Add Audit Logging

Log setiap access attempt ke SQLite database: timestamp, UID, result (grant/deny), door open duration. Buat web dashboard sederhana (Flask) untuk view log. Export log ke CSV untuk analysis.

5
Fail-Safe vs Fail-Secure Demo

Demonstrasi: saat power diputus (simulate power outage), electromagnetic lock = fail-safe (unlock, orang bisa keluar). Bandingkan dengan fail-secure (tetap locked). Diskusikan: kapan pakai masing-masing? Tambahkan UPS untuk keep system running saat outage.

Sesi 3 — Environmental Monitoring Station

1
Wire Sensors ke Arduino

Hubungkan: DHT22 (temperature + humidity) ke digital pin 2. MQ-2 (smoke/gas) ke analog pin A0. Buzzer ke pin 8. LED red (alert) ke pin 9. LED green (normal) ke pin 10. LCD 16x2 I2C untuk display real-time.

2
Program Arduino

Install library DHT, LiquidCrystal_I2C. Program: (a) read DHT22 every 5 detik, (b) read MQ-2 continuously, (c) display temp/humidity/smoke level di LCD, (d) if temp > 30°C atau humidity > 70% atau smoke > threshold → buzzer + red LED, (e) else green LED.

3
Set Thresholds

Berdasarkan ASHRAE: temp 18-27°C (alert > 30°C), humidity 40-60% (alert > 70% atau < 30%). MQ-2 smoke threshold: baseline + 30%. Test: heat sensor dengan hair dryer, humidifier untuk humidity, korek api untuk smoke.

4
IoT Dashboard Integration

Kirim data ke ThingSpeak atau Blynk cloud via ESP8266 WiFi module (atau Arduino + Ethernet shield). Setup dashboard: real-time gauge (temp, humidity, smoke), historical graph, alert notification via email/Telegram bot jika threshold exceeded.

5
Laporan & Recommendations

Buat laporan: (a) sensor calibration data, (b) threshold justifikasi, (c) dashboard screenshot, (d) alert test results, (e) rekomendasi: integrate dengan HVAC control, add water leak sensor, connect ke fire suppression system, integrate dengan SIEM untuk centralized monitoring.

Sesi 4 — Network Cable Tapping Demo

1
Setup UTP Tapping Lab

Buat 2 VM: attacker + victim di same switch. Victim browse HTTP site. Attacker run Wireshark. Demo UTP wiretap: gunakan cable splitter / network tap device di UTP cable antara victim dan switch. Capture all traffic — demonstrate bahwa UTP transmit data in cleartext jika tidak encrypted.

2
ARP Spoofing Demo

Attacker run ettercap -T -M arp /victim// /gateway//. Victim traffic sekarang flows through attacker. Wireshark capture semua — passwords, cookies, images. Pelajaran: UTP cable + no encryption = sniffable. Mitigasi: encryption (HTTPS, SSH, VPN), dynamic ARP inspection, port security.

3
Fiber-Optic Security Demo

Demonstrasi: fiber-optic cable TIDAK bisa di-tap dengan mudah. (1) Tapping fiber memerlukan specialized equipment (fiber coupler/splitter), (2) bending fiber untuk tap menyebabkan signal loss yang detectable, (3) no electromagnetic emanation (tidak bisa sniff via antenna). Pelajaran: fiber = paling aman dari wiretap.

4
Cable Security Best Practices

Implementasi di lab: (a) run UTP di transparent conduit (visible damage detection), (b) label semua cables, (c) document cable infrastructure, (d) restrict access ke cabling pathways (locked server room, cable trays above ceiling sealed), (e) use armored cable di area prone to rodent, (f) redundant paths (no single point of failure).

5
Laporan & Comparison

Buat laporan: (a) UTP tap demo dengan Wireshark capture, (b) ARP spoofing impact analysis, (c) fiber-optic security comparison, (d) cable security best practices checklist, (e) recommendation: migrate critical links ke fiber, implement port security, encryption everywhere.

  • 5 types physical security controls — Preventive (door locks, guards), Detective (motion detectors, alarms, CCTV), Deterrent (warning signs), Recovery (DR, BC, backup), Compensating (hot sites, backup power).
  • Location & site architecture considerations — facility location, neighboring buildings, entrances, server room, emergency exits.
  • Physical barriers — multi-layer: External (fences, bollards, walls), Middle (turnstiles, checkpoints), Internal (doors, windows, grills, glass).
  • Security personnel — guards, supervisors, safety officers, CISO. Training areas: 11+ topics.
  • Physical locks — Mechanical (warded, tumbler), Digital (fingerprint, smart card, PIN), Electric/Electromagnetic (fail-safe vs fail-secure), Combination.
  • Concealed weapon detection — walkthrough metal detectors, handheld detectors, X-ray inspection systems.
  • Mantrap — interlocking doors, 4-step process, separates non-secure from secure areas.
  • Lighting — 4 types: Continuous, Standby, Movable, Emergency.
  • Power supply — 6 UPS types: Standby, Line-interactive, Standby online hybrid, Standby Ferro, Double conversion online, Delta conversion online.
  • Workplace security — reception area, server/backup security, critical assets, removable devices, portable mobile devices.
  • Network cable security — 4 types: UTP (most vulnerable), STP (medium), Fiber-optic (most secure), Coaxial (medium). Best practices: separate wiring, armored cable, transparent conduit, undergrounding.
  • Physical security policy — design considerations checklist, building protection, entry points, surveillance, asset inventory.
  • Environmental controls — HVAC (4 types: split, hybrid heat, duct-free, packaged), EMI shielding (metal coating, eddy currents), Hot/Cold aisles (airflow management).
  • Praktikum 4 sesi — Physical Security Audit, Access Control (RFID + Pi), Environmental Monitoring (Arduino + sensors), Cable Tapping Demo.
Key Takeaway: Physical security adalah basis dari semua information security. Tanpa physical security, semua technical controls (firewall, IDS, encryption) dapat di-bypass dengan physical access. Siapa pun yang bisa sentuh server = bisa install keylogger, clone disk, reset password via console. Defense-in-depth dimulai dari physical layer — multi-layer barriers, multiple lock types, surveillance, environmental controls, dan power redundancy.
