Bagian 01

Tujuan Pembelajaran

Mengapa compliance, policies, dan governance adalah integral bagi program keamanan informasi.

Compliance, policies, dan governance adalah integral bagi program keamanan informasi organisasi manapun. Organisasi perlu mematuhi standar regulasi tertentu untuk menjalankan bisnis. Pada saat yang sama, organisasi juga harus memiliki security policies dan governance yang kuat untuk memenuhi standar regulasi. Modul ini membahas aspek administratif dari keamanan jaringan organisasi.

Setelah menyelesaikan modul ini, Anda akan mampu:

Bagian 02

Regulatory Frameworks Compliance

Sekumpulan guidelines dan best practices yang harus diikuti organisasi untuk memenuhi kebutuhan regulasi.

Regulatory framework compliance adalah sekumpulan guidelines dan best practices yang ditetapkan agar organisasi mengikutinya sehingga memenuhi kebutuhan regulasi, meningkatkan proses, memperbaiki proteksi, dan mencapai tujuan lain berdasarkan industri dan tipe data yang dikelola. Sering diperlukan bagi organisasi untuk mematuhi beberapa jenis regulasi keamanan. Mematuhi regulatory frameworks adalah upaya kolaboratif antara pemerintah dan badan swasta untuk mendorong perbaikan cybersecurity secara sukarela/mandatori.

Regulatory compliance mencegah organisasi dari denda besar atau menjadi korban data breach. Kebanyakan organisasi mematuhi lebih dari satu regulatory framework. Menentukan framework, policies, dan controls mana yang paling kompatibel dengan tujuan compliance organisasi adalah tugas sulit.

4 Komponen Regulatory Framework Compliance
Gambar 2. 4 komponen regulatory compliance — Frameworks → Policies → Standards → Procedures/Guidelines, dari high-level ke low-level.

2.1 Mengapa Organisasi Perlu Compliance

🛡
Improved Security

IT security regulations dan standards meningkatkan overall security organisasi dengan memenuhi baseline regulatory requirements. Baseline requirements memastikan consistent data security.

💰
Minimized Losses

Improved security mencegah security breaches yang dapat menyebabkan losses, repair costs, legal fees, atau denda besar. Investasi compliance jauh lebih murah dari biaya breach.

🤝
Maintenance of Trust

Data breaches menyebabkan perusahaan kehilangan reputasi dan trust dari customer. Compliance membuat customer percaya bahwa informasi mereka aman di organisasi.

⚡
Increased Control

Security organisasi meningkat dengan controls seperti mencegah kesalahan karyawan, implementasi strong credential systems & encryption, atau monitoring outside threats.

Bagian 03

PCI DSS — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard

Standar keamanan informasi proprietary untuk organisasi yang menangani cardholder information.

PCI DSS adalah proprietary information security standard untuk organisasi yang menangani cardholder information untuk major debit, credit, prepaid, e-purse, ATM, dan POS cards. Standar ini menawarkan comprehensive standards dan supporting materials untuk meningkatkan payment card data security. PCI DSS berlaku untuk semua entitas yang terlibat dalam payment card processing — merchants, processors, acquirers, issuers, dan service providers — serta semua entitas yang store, process, atau transmit cardholder data.

PCI DSS — 6 Requirements & 12 Sub-Requirements
Gambar 3. PCI DSS 6 requirements + 12 sub-requirements — framework komprehensif untuk melindungi cardholder data.

3.1 Contoh PCI DSS Requirements & Controls

PCI DSS RequirementPolicies, Procedures, Controls
1.1.1 Formal process for approving and testing all network connections and changes to firewall/router configurationsProvision for detecting all unauthorized network connections to/from IT assets
1.2.1 Restrict inbound and outbound traffic to that which is necessary for cardholder data environment, specifically deny all other trafficProvision for detecting unauthorized network connections
1.1.6 Documentation and business justification for use of all services, protocols, and ports allowedProvision for looking insecure protocols and services running on systems
1.3.1 Implement DMZ to limit inbound traffic to only authorized publicly accessible servicesProvision for checking how traffic flows across DMZ to/from internal network
5.1 Deploy anti-virus software on all systems commonly affected by malicious softwareProvision for detecting malware infection when anti-virus protection is disabled
Konsekuensi Non-Compliance: Failure to meet PCI DSS requirements dapat mengakibatkan denda $5,000–$100,000 per bulan dari card brands (Visa, Mastercard), atau termination of payment-card processing privileges — yang berarti organisasi tidak bisa menerima pembayaran kartu. Bagi e-commerce, ini = mati bisnis.
Bagian 04

HIPAA — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

Proteksi federal untuk individually identifiable health information — Privacy Rule, Security Rule, dan Enforcement Rule.

HIPAA Privacy Rule menyediakan proteksi federal untuk individually identifiable health information yang dipegang oleh covered entities dan business associates, serta memberi pasien array of rights terhadap informasi tersebut. Pada saat yang sama, Privacy Rule mengizinkan disclosure health information yang diperlukan untuk patient care dan tujuan necessary lainnya. Security Rule menentukan series administrative, physical, dan technical safeguards untuk covered entities dan business associates guna memastikan confidentiality, integrity, dan availability electronic protected health information (ePHI).

4.1 HIPAA Administrative Simplification Rules

📋
Electronic Transactions & Code Set Standards

HIPAA menetapkan covered entities: health plans, health care clearinghouses, dan health care providers. Standard transactions untuk EDI: claims, payment, claim status, eligibility, enrollment, referrals, coordination of benefits, premium payment.

🔒
Privacy Rule

Menetapkan standar nasional untuk melindungi medical records dan personal health information. Berlaku untuk health plans, clearinghouses, providers yang conduct electronic transactions. Memberi pasien rights: examine, obtain copy, request corrections.

🛡
Security Rule

Standar nasional untuk melindungi electronic personal health information yang created, received, used, atau maintained oleh covered entity. Memerlukan administrative, physical, dan technical safeguards untuk confidentiality, integrity, security ePHI.

🆔
Employer & NPI Standards

Employer Identifier: setiap employer punya standard national number. NPI (National Provider Identifier): 10-position, intelligence-free numeric identifier untuk covered health care providers.

HIPAA Security Rule — 3 Safeguard Categories
Gambar 4. HIPAA Security Rule 3 safeguard categories — Administrative, Physical, Technical. Compliance memerlukan ketiganya.
HIPAA Enforcement Rule: Berisi provisions terkait compliance, investigation, dan imposition of civil monetary penalties untuk violations. Denda HIPAA dapat mencapai $50,000 per violation (annual cap $1.5M per identical provision). Violations criminal dapat berupa 1-10 tahun penjara tergantung intent dan harm.
Bagian 05

Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX) — 2002

Akti untuk melindungi publik dan investor dengan meningkatkan akurasi dan reliability corporate disclosures.

SOX dienact tahun 2002, bertujuan melindungi publik dan investor dengan meningkatkan accuracy dan reliability corporate disclosures. Act ini tidak menjelaskan bagaimana organisasi harus menyimpan records, tetapi mendeskripsikan records yang organisasi harus simpan dan durasi penyimpanan. SOX mandated beberapa reforms untuk enhance corporate responsibility, enhance financial disclosures, dan combat corporate dan accounting fraud. Key requirements diorganisasi ke dalam 11 titles.

5.1 11 Titles SOX

I
PCAOB

Public Company Accounting Oversight Board — independent oversight of public accounting firms. Register audit services, define compliance audit processes, inspect conduct, enforce compliance.

II
Auditor Independence

Standar external auditor independence, limit conflicts of interest. Auditor approval, audit partner rotation, restrict non-audit services (consulting) untuk same clients.

III
Corporate Responsibility

Senior executives take individual responsibility untuk accuracy & completeness corporate financial reports. Specific forfeitures of benefits dan civil penalties untuk non-compliance.

IV
Enhanced Financial Disclosures

Enhanced reporting: off-balance-sheet transactions, pro-forma figures, stock transactions corporate officers. Internal controls untuk accuracy financial reports + audits on those controls.

V-VI
Analyst Conflicts & SEC Authority

Title V: code of conduct securities analysts, disclose conflicts. Title VI: SEC authority censure/bar securities professionals, conditions to bar broker/advisor/dealer.

VII-XI
Studies, Fraud, Tax, Accountability

VII: studies & reports. VIII: Corporate & Criminal Fraud Accountability (whistleblower protection). IX: White-Collar Crime Penalty Enhancement. X: CEO sign tax return. XI: Corporate Fraud Accountability.

SOX Section 404 (paling terkenal): Memerlukan management dan external auditor untuk melaporkan adequacy internal control over financial reporting (ICFR). Ini adalah bagian paling expensive dari SOX compliance — memerlukan dokumentasi extensive, testing, dan audit internal controls. Violation: hingga 20 tahun penjara untuk executive yang certify false financial reports.
Bagian 06

GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018

Toughest privacy and security law di dunia — 7 principles + harsh fines untuk violators.

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) adalah toughest privacy and security law di dunia. Meskipun didraft dan dipass oleh European Union (EU), GDPR memberlakukan obligations ke organisasi di manapun, asalkan mereka target atau collect data related to people in the EU. Regulasi ini efektif sejak 25 Mei 2018. GDPR akan mengenakan denda keras terhadap mereka yang melanggar privacy dan security standards, dengan penalties mencapai puluhan juta euro.

GDPR — 7 Data Protection Principles (Article 5)
Gambar 5. GDPR 7 principles — Lawfulness, Purpose, Minimization, Accuracy, Storage, Integrity, Accountability.

6.1 GDPR Fines & Penalties

💰
Tier 1 — Lower Level

Hingga €10 juta atau 2% annual global turnover (mana yang lebih tinggi). Untuk violations terkait: data subject rights, DPIA, records of processing, security obligations, breach notification, data protection by design.

💰
Tier 2 — Upper Level

Hingga €20 juta atau 4% annual global turnover (mana yang lebih tinggi). Untuk violations terkait: basic principles, data subject rights, international transfers, non-compliance dengan order regulator.

6.2 Data Protection Act 2018 (UK)

DPA 2018 menetapkan framework untuk data protection law di UK. Update dan replace Data Protection Act 1998, efektif 25 Mei 2018. Diupdate 1 Januari 2021 untuk reflect UK's status outside EU (post-Brexit). DPA protects individuals dengan regard to processing personal data — requiring lawful & fair processing, conferring rights on data subject (obtain info, rectify inaccurate data), dan conferring functions on Information Commissioner.

GDPR Indonesia Equivalent: Indonesia mengesahkan UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP) yang efektif 17 Oktober 2024. Mirip GDPR: 7 principles (legitimate purpose, data accuracy, data minimization, transparency, etc.), denda hingga 2% dari annual revenue, dan wajib appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO). Organisasi Indonesia wajib comply — bukan opsional.
Bagian 07

ISO/IEC 27000 Series — Information Security Standards

Suite standar keamanan informasi paling komprehensif di dunia — dari ISMS hingga cloud, forensics, dan healthcare.

ISO/IEC 27000 series adalah keluarga standar keamanan informasi yang paling banyak diadopsi secara global. Standar ini menyediakan framework untuk Information Security Management System (ISMS) dan guidelines untuk berbagai aspek keamanan informasi. Berikut adalah standar-standar utama dalam seri 27000.

ISO/IEC 27000 Series — Standar Utama
Gambar 6. ISO/IEC 27000 series — 12+ standar utama untuk berbagai aspek information security management.
ISO 27001 Certification: ISO 27001 adalah standar yang dapat certified — organisasi dapat audit dan dapat certificate dari accredited certification body (BSI, DNV, TÜV, dll). Certificate valid 3 tahun, surveillance audit per tahun. Manfaat: tender requirement, customer trust, regulatory compliance evidence, continuous improvement framework.
Bagian 08

FISMA & Other Information Security Acts

FISMA, USA PATRIOT Act, FOIA, ECPA, Computer Fraud and Abuse Act — dan regulasi keamanan lainnya.

8.1 Federal Information Security Management Act (FISMA)

FISMA 2002 dienact untuk menghasilkan beberapa key security standards dan guidelines yang required oleh Congressional legislation. FISMA menyediakan comprehensive framework untuk memastikan effectiveness information security controls over information resources yang support federal operations dan assets. FISMA memerlukan setiap federal agency untuk develop, document, dan implement agency-wide program untuk information security.

📋
FISMA Framework Includes

• Standards untuk categorizing information systems by mission impact
• Standards untuk minimum security requirements
• Guidance untuk selecting appropriate security controls
• Guidance untuk assessing security controls effectiveness
• Guidance untuk security authorization of information systems

🏛
NIST Publications

FISMA diimplementasi via NIST publications: FIPS 199 (categorization), FIPS 200 (minimum requirements), SP 800-53 (security controls catalog), SP 800-37 (RMF — Risk Management Framework), SP 800-60 (system categorization guide).

8.2 Other Information Security Acts

🇺🇸
USA PATRIOT Act 2001

Tujuan: deter dan punish terrorist acts di U.S. dan dunia, enhance law enforcement investigatory tools. Strengthen measures prevent/detect/prosecute international money laundering & terrorism financing.

📄
Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)

Memberi public right untuk request access to records dari federal agency. "Law yang keeps citizens informed about their government." 9 exemptions protect personal privacy, national security, law enforcement.

📱
Electronic Communications Privacy Act (ECPA) 1986

Update Federal Wiretap Act 1968 yang tidak cover computer & digital communications. Bersama Stored Wire Electronic Communications Act. USA PATRIOT Act clarify & update ECPA untuk new technologies.

⚖
Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) 1986

Amendment ke Counterfeit Access Device and Abuse Act 1984. "Whoever intentionally accesses a computer without authorization atau exceeds authorized access" shall be punished. Criminal + civil actions.

🎵
Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)

American copyright law implementing 2 WIPO treaties 1996. Legal prohibitions against circumvention of technological protection measures. 5 titles: WIPO Implementation, Online Infringement, Computer Maintenance, Misc, Vessel Hull Design.

🏦
Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA)

US federal law requiring financial institutions explain how they share & protect customer private information. Financial Privacy Rules + Safeguards Rules. Penalty: $100K per violation + officers personally liable $10K + 5 years prison.

Indonesia Cyber Law Equivalents: UU ITE (No. 11/2008, amended No. 19/2016, No. 1/2024) — Informasi dan Transaksi Elektronik. UU PDP (No. 27/2022) — Perlindungan Data Pribadi (GDPR equivalent). UU Cyber Security — sedang dalam pembahasan. Permen Kominfo tentang Penyelenggaraan Sistem Elektronik. Pelanggaran: pidana + denda + sanksi administratif.

Bagian 10

Security Policy — Basics & Characteristics

Well-documented set of plans, processes, procedures, standards, dan guidelines untuk establish ideal information security status.

Security policy adalah well-documented set of plans, processes, procedures, standards, dan guidelines yang diperlukan untuk establish ideal information security status organisasi. Security policies digunakan untuk inform people cara kerja yang safe dan secure; mereka define dan guide employee actions cara menangani organisasi sensitive operation, data, atau resources. Security policy adalah integral part dari information security management program untuk organisasi manapun.

Security policy accomplish 3 goals:

⚖
Reduce Legal Liability

Reduce atau eliminate legal liability ke employees dan third parties. Policy yang documented adalah defense di court.

🔒
Protect Confidential Info

Protect confidential dan proprietary information dari theft, misuse, unauthorized disclosure, atau modification.

⚡
Prevent Resource Waste

Prevent computing resource waste — optimasi usage, prevent abuse, monitor efficiency.

10.1 8 Characteristics of a Good Security Policy

8 Karakteristik Good Security Policy
Gambar 7. 8 karakteristik good security policy — dari concise hingga legal compliance. Policy tanpa karakteristik ini akan gagal implementasi.

10.2 Typical Policy Document Content

📋
Document Control

Document Control (store/modify/distribute), Document Location, Revision History, Version Number, Approvals, Distribution, Document History. Tracking & governance dokumen policy.

📖

Overview (background info), Purpose (detailed explanation why), Scope (who & what covered), Definitions (terms used), Roles & Responsibilities, Target Audience.

📝

Policies (statements on each aspect), Sanctions & Violations (allow/deny processes), Related Standards/Policies/Processes, Contact Information, Where to Find More Information, Glossary/Acronyms.

🔧

High-level Security Requirements (discipline, safeguard, procedural, assurance), Policy Description, Security Concept of Operation, Allocation of Security Enforcement to architecture elements.

Bagian 11

Types of Information Security Policies

3 tipe utama (EISP, ISSP, SSSP) + 4 kategori internet access policies.

11.1 3 Tipe Utama Security Policies

E
EISP — Enterprise Info Security Policy

Drives organisasi's scope dan provides direction ke security policies. Support dengan offering ideology, purpose, methods untuk create secure environment. Examples: application policy, network device security policy, security policy auditing, backup/restore policy, system security policy.

I
ISSP — Issue Specific Security Policy

Directs audience on usage technology-based systems dengan guidelines. Address specific security issues. Specifies necessary technologies + preventive measures: authorization, privacy protection, fair & responsible use. Examples: remote access, wireless, incident response, password, personal devices, internet usage.

S
SSSP — System Specific Security Policy

Directs users saat configuring atau maintaining a system. Focus pada overall security particular system. Addresses implementation & configuration technology + user behavior. Examples: DMZ policy, encryption policy, acceptable use, secure cloud computing, IDS/IPS, access control policy.

11.2 4 Internet Access Policies

4 Internet Access Policies — dari Promiscuous ke Paranoid
Gambar 8. 4 internet access policies — Promiscuous (no rules) → Permissive (block known bad) → Prudent (default deny) → Paranoid (forbid all). Prudent = best practice.
Bagian 12

Password Policy

Set of rules untuk increase system security dengan encouraging strong passwords.

Password policy adalah set of rules untuk increase system security dengan encouraging users menggunakan strong passwords untuk access organisasi's resources dan keep them secure. Tujuan policy adalah protect organisasi's resources dengan creating robust protected passwords. Policy statement harus include standard practice untuk creating robust password.

12.1 Password Policy Components

📏
Password Length & Formation

Length 8-14 characters. Formation: one or more numerical digits, special characters (@, #, $), uppercase & lowercase letters, avoid personal info, no company name. Case sensitive (username mungkin tidak).

🚫
Password Blacklists

List of words prohibited from use as passwords karena familiarity. Help prevent usage common passwords. Contoh: "password", "123456", "qwerty", "admin", company name, common names.

⏰
Password Duration

Suggest users change passwords regularly — usually every 90 or 180 days. Maximum password age: 60 days. Minimum password age: no limit. Password history: old passwords cannot be reused.

🔑
Common Password Practices

Don't share account details, don't keep common password for all accounts, don't share passwords, never write password anywhere, don't communicate via email/phone/IM, don't leave machine unattended (lock/logoff), different passwords for OS & applications.

Update NIST SP 800-63B (2017): NIST terbaru merekomendasikan: (1) minimum 8 char,鼓励 passphrase panjang 64 char; (2) NO forced periodic rotation (rotation justru menurunkan security); (3) check against breach databases; (4) accept semua karakter termasuk emoji; (5) rate limiting + lockout. Update policy Anda sesuai standar terbaru.
Bagian 13

Security & Awareness Training

4 tipe training utama: Security Policy, Physical Security, Social Engineering, Data Classification.

Employee dan user training memainkan peran penting dalam governance overall security organisasi. Untrained employee atau user dapat pose considerable risk ke organisasi. Oleh karena itu, penting untuk membuat mereka aware tentang security policies, dan conduct awareness training programs lain untuk maintain organisasi security.

13.1 Employee Awareness & Training — Security Policy

📚
Why Policy Training?

Security policy training teaches employees how perform duties dan comply dengan security policy. Train new employees sebelum grant akses ke network. Help employees recognize & respond security threats real-time. Make aware new updates probable vulnerabilities.

✓
Advantages

• Effective implementation of security policy
• Policies followed, not just enforced
• Creates awareness on compliance issues
• Helps organization enhance network security
• Early identification of breach decreases cost

13.2 Physical Security Training

Well-trained dan skilled personnel dapat minimize risk physical security threat. Training harus educate employees tentang: minimize breaches, identify elements prone hardware theft, assess risks handling sensitive data, ensure physical security at workplace.

13.3 Social Engineering Training

Social Engineering Attack Awareness & Training
Gambar 9. Social engineering awareness training — 4 area risiko utama + teknik tambahan yang harus dikenali employee.

13.4 Data Classification Training

Organisasi harus train employees cara mengetahui apakah informasi confidential. Security labels digunakan untuk mark security level requirements untuk information assets dan controls access ke mereka. Security labels categorize berdasarkan siapa yang dapat access data atau object.

5 Data Classification Levels (Security Labels)
Gambar 10. 5 level data classification — Unclassified → Restricted → Confidential → Secret → Top Secret. Setiap level = clearance + controls berbeda.
Metode Training: Classroom style training · Online training · Round table discussions · Security awareness website · Providing hints · Making short films · Seminars · Simulation employee training · Hands-on training · Lectures · Coaching/mentoring · Case studies · Management specific activities · Group discussions. Kombinasi metode = retention lebih baik.

Modul Praktikum Administrative Controls

Empat sesi praktik hands-on: compliance gap analysis, security policy drafting, phishing simulation, dan data classification implementation.

14.1 Tujuan Praktikum

1
Compliance Analysis

Melakukan gap analysis PCI DSS atau ISO 27001 pada case study organisasi fiktif — identifikasi gap & rekomendasi remediasi.

2
Policy Drafting

Menyusun draft security policy (Acceptable Use Policy + Password Policy) lengkap dengan 8 karakteristik good policy.

3
Phishing Simulation

Menjalankan phishing simulation campaign menggunakan GoPhish — measure click rate, train response.

4
Data Classification

Implementasi data classification scheme pada file system — label files, set access controls per classification level.

14.4 Langkah Praktikum

Sesi 1 — Compliance Gap Analysis (PCI DSS atau ISO 27001)

1
Pilih Case Study Organisasi

Buat profil organisasi fiktif: "TechMart" — e-commerce dengan 500K customer, process credit card, 100 karyawan. Identifikasi data yang di-handle: cardholder data, PII customer, employee data. Tentukan framework: PCI DSS (karena process credit card).

2
Download PCI DSS v4.0 Requirements

Download dari pcisecuritystandards.org. Baca 12 requirements utama. Buat spreadsheet dengan kolom: Requirement #, Description, Status (Compliant/Gap/Partial), Evidence, Remediation Plan.

3
Assess Setiap Requirement

Untuk setiap requirement, assess apakah TechMart comply. Contoh: Req 1.1 (firewall) — apakah ada? Req 3 (protect stored cardholder data) — apakah encrypted? Req 8 (unique ID) — apakah ada MFA? Catat gaps yang ditemukan.

4
Prioritize Gaps

Rate setiap gap berdasarkan: (a) risk severity (Critical/High/Medium/Low), (b) effort to remediate (Low/Medium/High), (c) cost. Buat remediation roadmap: Quick wins (90 hari), Medium-term (6 bulan), Long-term (1 tahun).

5
Laporan Gap Analysis

Buat laporan: executive summary (untuk C-level), detailed findings (per requirement), risk matrix, remediation roadmap dengan timeline + budget estimate. Sertakan evidence (screenshot, config sample).

Sesi 2 — Security Policy Drafting

1
Pilih Policy untuk Draft

Draft 2 policy: (1) Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) — bagaimana employee menggunakan IT resources; (2) Password Policy — standar password di organisasi.

2
Struktur Dokumen Policy

Gunakan struktur standar: Document Control, Revision History, Overview, Purpose, Scope, Definitions, Roles & Responsibilities, Target Audience, Policies (statements), Sanctions & Violations, Related Standards, Contact Info, Glossary.

3
Draft AUP Content

Tulis: (a) acceptable use (email, internet, software), (b) unacceptable use (piracy, hacking, personal business), (c) monitoring disclaimer, (d) no expectation of privacy, (e) reporting violations, (f) sanctions (verbal warning → termination → legal action).

4
Draft Password Policy

Tulis: (a) minimum 12 char (NIST 800-63B), (b) no forced rotation (breach-based instead), (c) breach check via HIBP, (d) MFA required for admin/remote, (e) password manager encouraged, (f) account lockout after 5 failed, (g) storage: Argon2/bcrypt.

5
Review & Presentation

Review policy terhadap 8 characteristics good policy (concise, usable, feasible, etc.). Present ke peer review — dapat feedback. Final draft dengan version control, approval workflow, distribution list.

Sesi 3 — Phishing Simulation dengan GoPhish

1
Install GoPhish

Download dari getgophish.com. Run: ./gophish. Akses https://localhost:3333 (admin: gophish/gophish). Ganti password admin. Setup SMTP relay (boleh Mailtrap untuk test).

2
Import Email Targets

Buat CSV dengan 10-15 email target (kawan kelas, email pribadi — dengan consent). Upload ke GoPhish → Users & Groups. Buat group "Test-Employees".

3
Design Phishing Template

Buat email template "Your password will expire" — impersonate IT department. Landing page: fake login form yang capture credentials (untuk demo). Penting: jangan benar-benar capture credentials — gunakan educational landing page yang langsung bilang "This was a phishing simulation".

4
Launch Campaign

Launch campaign ke group "Test-Employees". Tunggu 24-48 jam. Monitor dashboard: email sent, email opened, link clicked, credentials submitted (harusnya tidak ada karena educational landing page).

5
Analisis & Laporan

Catat metrics: open rate, click rate, report rate (berapa yang report ke IT). Buat laporan: campaign summary, per-user result, rekomendasi training untuk yang click. Diskusikan: cara improve — quarterly simulation, targeted training, reward reporting.

Sesi 4 — Data Classification Implementation

1
Define Classification Scheme

Adopsi 5 level: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted, Top Secret. Buat matrix: data type → classification. Contoh: marketing brochure = Public, internal memo = Internal, salary data = Confidential, trade secret = Restricted, merger plan = Top Secret.

2
Create Labeling System

Di Linux/Windows, buat labeling convention: filename prefix [PUB], [INT], [CONF], [REST], [TS]. Atau gunakan metadata: file properties → classification tag. Buat script Python untuk auto-label berdasarkan content keyword.

3
Set Access Controls per Level

Implementasi: Public = all read; Internal = all employees read; Confidential = department read; Restricted = need-to-know; Top Secret = named individuals. Gunakan ACL: setfacl -m g:finance:rw--- salary_data.xlsx untuk Confidential finance-only.

4
DLP Proof of Concept

Install openDLP atau buat script sederhana yang scan file system untuk classification labels. Setup alert: jika file [REST] di-copy ke USB → alert. Jika file [TS] di-email → block. Dokumentasikan DLP rules.

5
Laporan & Recommendations

Buat laporan: classification scheme, labeling convention, access control matrix, DLP rule examples. Rekomendasi: training untuk semua employee, periodic audit, automated discovery tool, integration dengan SIEM.

14.5 Lembar Kerja / Tugas

Q1
Compliance Priority

Dari Sesi 1, identifikasi 3 gap paling critical di TechMart. Mengapa gap tersebut critical? Apa dampak bisnis jika tidak diperbaiki? Berapa estimasi biaya remediasi vs biaya breach?

Q2
Policy Trade-off

Dari Sesi 2, jelaskan trade-off antara policy yang ketat (prudent) vs fleksibel (permissive). Kapan masing-masing cocok? Bagaimana memastikan employee comply tanpa menjadi paranoid?

Q3
Phishing Metrics

Dari Sesi 3, apa click rate campaign Anda? Bandingkan dengan industry average (15-25%). Apa faktor yang membuat user click? Bagaimana training dapat menurunkan click rate?

Q4
Classification Challenge

Dari Sesi 4, apa tantangan terbesar implementasi data classification? Bagaimana menangani data yang ambigu (e.g., email yang berisi info confidential + internal)? Apa otomatisasi yang mungkin?

Q5
Multi-Framework Compliance

Jika TechMart juga melayani customer EU dan US public company, framework apa lagi yang harus comply? Bagaimana mengelola overlap dan konflik antar framework?

Q6
Indonesia Context

Bagaimana UU PDP Indonesia (No. 27/2022) membandingkan dengan GDPR? Apa persamaan dan perbedaan utama? Apa implikasinya untuk organisasi Indonesia yang juga melayani customer EU?

14.6 Checklist Evaluasi

Praktikum dinyatakan lulus jika minimal 10 dari 12 kriteria berikut terpenuhi:

  • Gap analysis spreadsheet lengkap untuk 12 PCI DSS requirements
  • Minimal 5 gaps teridentifikasi dengan severity rating
  • Remediation roadmap dengan timeline + budget estimate
  • Draft AUP lengkap dengan 13 section standar
  • Draft Password Policy mengikuti NIST SP 800-63B (2017+)
  • Policy melewati review 8 characteristics good policy
  • GoPhish terinstall dan campaign sukses launch
  • Minimal 10 target email dalam campaign
  • Phishing metrics (open rate, click rate) terdokumentasi
  • Classification scheme 5 level terdefinisi dengan contoh per level
  • Access control matrix per classification level implemented
  • DLP proof-of-concept atau rule examples terdokumentasi
Bonus: Mahasiswa yang dapat mendemonstrasikan: (a) integrasi policy dengan HR onboarding system, (b) automated compliance dashboard menggunakan GRC tool (like Drata, Vanta), (c) phishing simulation dengan scenario spear-phishing customized per department, atau (d) implementasi classification di cloud (AWS Macie / Azure Purview) — akan mendapat nilai tambahan.

Perjalanan Pembelajaran Modul 03 — Dari Compliance ke Training
Gambar 12. Ringkasan perjalanan Modul 03 — dari compliance frameworks hingga awareness training.
  • Regulatory framework compliance — 4 komponen: Frameworks, Policies, Standards, Procedures/Guidelines.
  • Mengapa organisasi perlu compliance — Improved Security, Minimized Losses, Trust Maintenance, Increased Control.
  • PCI DSS — 6 requirements + 12 sub-requirements untuk protect cardholder data. Non-compliance = denda + termination.
  • HIPAA — Privacy Rule, Security Rule (3 safeguards: Administrative, Physical, Technical), Enforcement Rule. NPI 10-digit identifier.
  • SOX (2002) — 11 titles untuk protect investors via accurate corporate disclosures. Section 404 = ICFR (paling expensive).
  • GLBA — Financial institutions privacy rules + safeguards rules. Penalty $100K per violation + officers liable.
  • GDPR (2018) — 7 principles: Lawfulness, Purpose Limitation, Data Minimization, Accuracy, Storage Limitation, Integrity, Accountability. Fines up to €20M / 4% global turnover.
  • DPA 2018 (UK) — UK's GDPR implementation post-Brexit. Information Commissioner oversight.
  • ISO/IEC 27000 series — 27001 (ISMS), 27002 (controls), 27005 (risk), 27017/18 (cloud), 27031 (BC), 27035 (incident), 27037 (forensics), 27799 (healthcare).
  • DMCA — 5 titles implementing WIPO treaties. Anti-circumvention + safe harbor for service providers.
  • FISMA — Federal agencies security framework via NIST SP 800-53. Categorization, controls, assessment, authorization.
  • Other acts — USA PATRIOT Act, FOIA, ECPA, CFAA, Human Rights Act, Computer Misuse Act.
  • Cyber law per negara — US, UK, Australia, China, India, Germany, Japan, Singapore, Indonesia (UU ITE + UU PDP), Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA).
  • Security policy — 3 goals (reduce liability, protect confidential, prevent waste). 8 characteristics (concise, usable, feasible, understandable, realistic, consistent, procedurally tolerable, legal compliance).
  • 3 tipe policy — EISP (enterprise), ISSP (issue-specific), SSSP (system-specific).
  • 4 internet access policies — Promiscuous, Permissive, Prudent (best practice), Paranoid.
  • Password policy — components (length, blacklist, duration, common practices). NIST 800-63B update: 12+ char, no forced rotation, breach check.
  • Security awareness training — 4 tipe: Security Policy, Physical Security, Social Engineering (4 attack areas), Data Classification (5 levels: Unclassified → Top Secret).
  • Praktikum 4 sesi — Compliance Gap Analysis, Policy Drafting, GoPhish Phishing Simulation, Data Classification Lab.
Key Takeaway: Administrative controls adalah pondasi governance keamanan informasi. Tanpa compliance, policy, dan training yang tepat, semua technical controls (firewall, IDS, encryption) menjadi tidak efektif — karena manusia dan proses adalah weakest link. Compliance is not optional, policy is not paperwork, training is not optional. Modul ini adalah jembatan antara technical security dan business risk management.
